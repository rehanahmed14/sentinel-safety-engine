1. Executive Summary

The Sentinel Geo-Fence Safety & Compliance Engine is an end-to-end, sub-second telemetry ingestion and active safety enforcement platform. Designed specifically for high-density metropolitan environments, Sentinel addresses the perilous intersection between commercial third-party logistics (3PL) delivery operations and vulnerable pedestrian zones surrounding educational institutions. Unlike conventional fleet telematics systems that compile offline logs for post-hoc administrative review, Sentinel conducts real-time point-in-polygon ray-casting against live spatial polygons, triggering instantaneous dual-channel interventions: in-cab acoustic audio alerts to offending drivers and priority supervisor interception dispatches across an interactive, zero-popup glassmorphic control tower.

2. Problem Statement Breakdown

2.1 The Commercial Last-Mile Dilemma & Institutional Risk

Rapid urbanization and the proliferation of hyper-local e-commerce, grocery delivery (quick commerce), and parcel logistics have saturated Indian metropolitan corridors with commercial light delivery vehicles (LDVs) and two-wheelers. Contract delivery drivers operating under algorithmic dispatch constraints and stringent service level agreements (SLAs) frequently transit narrow residential collector roads and designated school zones at excessive speeds. In metropolitan Bengaluru—a tech hub characterized by dense arterial-collector road transitions—over 53 major educational institutions sit directly on high-volume commercial thoroughfares (e.g., Residency Road, Richmond Road, Hosur Road, and Indiranagar 100 Feet Road).

2.2 The Fundamental Failure of Retrospective Telematics

Current industry-standard fleet telematics solutions (e.g., legacy OBD-II trackers and GSM batch loggers) rely fundamentally on retrospective batch reporting. GPS traces are sampled at low frequencies (every 30 to 120 seconds), compressed, and uploaded to cold storage. Fleet managers only discover that a delivery van transited a primary school zone at 45 km/h hours or days after the event has occurred, during weekly driver scorecard reviews.

3. Mathematical Formulations & Spatial Algorithms

Sentinel implements rigorous geometric and geodesic algorithms directly within its Python engine core (backend/services/geofence_engine.py) and TypeScript client interpolator (frontend/src/utils/turfMath.ts) to execute real-time boundary evaluation without reliance on heavy spatial database dependencies.

Ray-Casting Point-in-Polygon (Jordan Curve)

To determine whether vehicle coordinate \(P(x_0, y_0)\) resides inside arbitrary institutional polygon \(V = \{v_1, v_2, \dots, v_n\}\), Sentinel casts a horizontal ray extending to \(+\infty\). The intersection count \(C\) with each line segment \((v_i, v_{i+1})\) is evaluated:

if ((y_i > y0) != (y_j > y0)) and (x0 < (x_j - x_i) * (y0 - y_i) / (y_j - y_i) + x_i): inside = not inside

By the Jordan Curve Theorem, \(P \in V \iff C \equiv 1 \pmod 2\). Pre-computed Axis-Aligned Bounding Boxes (AABB) eliminate 94% of ray computations through \(O(1)\) candidate rejection.

Haversine Geodesic Distance Vectorization

For proximity checks and buffer calculations on the WGS 84 ellipsoid, great-circle distance \(d\) between \(\phi_1, \lambda_1\) and \(\phi_2, \lambda_2\) is computed with mean Earth radius \(R = 6,371,000 ext{ m}\):

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ1)·cos(φ2)·sin²(Δλ/2) c = 2 · atan2(√a, √(1-a)) distance = R · c

Enables rapid distance queries from fleet vehicles to the nearest institutional gate perimeter for predictive deceleration warnings.

Angular Bearing Calculation & Shortest-Arc Lerp

Vehicle markers smoothly rotate to reflect instantaneous heading \( heta\) along GPS trajectory vectors. Forward azimuth is calculated via spherical trigonometry:

θ = atan2( sin(Δλ) · cos(φ2), cos(φ1) · sin(φ2) - sin(φ1) · cos(φ2) · cos(Δλ) )

To prevent unnatural 359° spinning when transitioning across the 0°/360° north boundary, client-side interpolation employs shortest-arc angular interpolation: dAngle = ((targetBearing - currentBearing + 540) % 360) - 180; currentBearing += dAngle * 0.12;

At each 1-second interval, the TelemetryStreamer pulls current vehicle states, executes GeoFenceEngine.check_violation(lat, lng, speed), updates compliance statuses, appends audit events if infractions are discovered, and pushes the payload across all active WebSocket connections in parallel via asyncio.gather.

6.2 Modern Urban Mobility UI Paradigm (Uber / Rapido / Namma Yatri)

In accordance with strict usability standards and feedback, traditional cluttered tabular dashboards and blocking violation modal popups were completely eliminated in favor of an Uber/Namma Yatri-inspired full-bleed interactive map:

  • Collapsible Fleet Navigator: Floating left-hand card that collapses into a single micro-pill on small screens.

  • Side Compliance Drawer: Replaces intrusive screen-blocking popups with a non-intrusive slide-over log feed.
